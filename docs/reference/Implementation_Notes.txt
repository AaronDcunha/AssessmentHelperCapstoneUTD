and whether postponement is supported.

5. EVALUATION ELIGIBILITY - FREQUENCY AND SIGNUP DEADLINE CONFIRMED
Assistant professors require evaluation every year. Associate/full professors
require evaluation every two years. A professor due in a particular semester
must sign up by that semester's evaluation-cycle signup deadline. For example,
a professor due in Fall 2026 must sign up by the Fall 2026 signup deadline.
Signing up does not itself mean the observation has been completed; observation
completion is tracked separately against the observation deadline.
Remaining edge cases: faculty without evaluation history, missing hire dates,
and rank changes. Retain the first-semester exclusion and voluntary signup
behavior described in UC2, and specify how they are checked.

6. KPI DEFINITIONS - NEEDS CLARIFICATION
Agree on each metric's numerator, denominator, reporting period, and treatment
of missing data before implementation.
- Evaluation Eligibility Accuracy: clarify whether this measures faculty
  evaluation eligibility or observer matching correctness.
- Assessment Participation Rate: decide how voluntary signups affect the rate.
- Observer Utilization Rate: distinguish accepting invitations from being
  selected as the final observer; count distinct professors consistently.
- List Sufficiency Rate: clarify whether this is the percentage receiving five
  candidates or the average candidate count per signup. The current formula
  is ambiguous.
- Missing Observation Count: distinguish an observation that did not occur
  from an observation whose assessment form has not yet been submitted.
- Survey Completion Rate: clarify the eligible population and prevent double
  counting professors participating as both observer and observee.
- Overdue Evaluation Count: use the agreed due/overdue eligibility rule.
Clarify whether any provisional KPIs appear during the cycle; final KPIs are
expected after the feedback deadline, while operational reports remain live.
